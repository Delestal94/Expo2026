# Spec Delta: Exhibitors & B2B Business Rounds

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bioceanic Matchmaking Portal
The web application SHALL provide a dedicated B2B matchmaking portal at `/rondas-b2b`.

#### Scenario: User reserves 20-min bilateral meeting slot
- **GIVEN** an accredited representative on `/rondas-b2b`
- **WHEN** selecting an available 20-minute meeting slot
- **THEN** a meeting reservation confirmation with company compatibility score is generated.

# Proposal: Roadmap & Presentation Views for ExpoJuy 2026

## Intent & Context

We are participating in the **Desafío Digital ExpoJuy 2026**, organized by the **Dirección Provincial de Servicios Basados en el Conocimiento** (Ministerio de Desarrollo Económico y Producción de Jujuy), the **Cámara de Comercio Exterior de Jujuy**, and **Clustear**.

### Key Contest Milestones
- **September 8, 2026 (23:59 ART)**: Phase 0 Submission deadline (Navigable mockup/prototype + Memoria Descriptiva).
- **September 11, 2026**: Shortlist announcement.
- **September 14, 2026**: Demo Day evaluation and winner announcement ($750.000 prize + official development contract).
- **September 30, 2026**: Final platform delivery for production deployment.
- **October 9–12, 2026**: ExpoJuy 2026 live event at Ciudad Cultural, San Salvador de Jujuy.

## Problem Statement
The current prototype has a solid functional home/landing with `StrataCanvas`, `VenueMap`, and `Directory`/`MatchingPreview` on `https://expojuy2026.vercel.app`. However, to secure the evaluation at Demo Day and present an undeniable competitive advantage, we must present the **complete visual roadmap and interactive views** demonstrating technical feasibility and user experience across all upcoming phases.

## Scope of Proposal
1. **Interactive Roadmap View (`/roadmap`)**: Interactive visual timeline detailing Phase 0 (Mockup & Spec), Phase 1 (Core Integration & Auth), Phase 2 (Live Event & RAG AI), and Phase 3 (Post-Event Analytics).
2. **Visitor Access & Digital QR Pass View (`/acceso` / `/ticketing`)**: Complete registration/ticketing UI mockup showing dynamic QR generation, turnstile scanning simulation, and admission mode toggle.
3. **B2B Business Rounds & Matchmaking View (`/rondas-b2b`)**: Bioceanic Corridor bilateral meeting scheduler UI (Argentina, Chile, Paraguay, Brazil) with 20-minute slot reservation.
4. **Multilingual Domain-Scoped AI Assistant View (`/asistente`)**: Interactive conversational UI demonstrating real-time RAG query answering in Spanish, English, Portuguese, and Mandarin with human fallback escalation.
5. **Interactive Fairgrounds Map Detailed View (`/mapa`)**: Fullscreen responsive vector map of Ciudad Cultural with pavilion inspection, live agenda timeline, and booth wayfinding.
6. **Figma / Stitch Presentation Assets**: Ready-to-export frames and structured prompts to generate presentation decks and Figma artifacts for the pitch.
